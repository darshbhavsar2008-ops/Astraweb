01.00 A different perspective

Technology should feel extraordinary. Not complicated.

Astra began with a simple idea: personal AI should be more than a text box on a screen. It should be an experience shaped by clarity, atmosphere, usefulness, and the freedom to explore ideas.

Every element of the Astra identity is built around a distinct visual language — deep space, precise geometry, restrained light, and an interface that makes room for the person using it.
